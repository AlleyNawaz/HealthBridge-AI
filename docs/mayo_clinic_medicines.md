# Mayo Clinic Over-the-Counter Medication & Symptom Care Guide

## 1. Antipyretics & Analgesics
- **Acetaminophen (Paracetamol)**: Dosage 500-1000 mg every 4-6 hours as needed (maximum 4000 mg/24 hours for adults). Do not combine with alcohol or hepatotoxic agents.
- **Ibuprofen (NSAID)**: Dosage 200-400 mg every 4-6 hours with food. Contraindicated in active peptic ulcer disease, severe renal failure, or third-trimester pregnancy.

## 2. Antihistamines & Oral Decongestants
- **Cetirizine / Loratadine**: Second-generation non-drowsy antihistamines for allergic rhinitis and urticaria.
- **Saline Nasal Irrigation**: Warm 0.9% sodium chloride saline rinses for acute sinonasal congestion.
