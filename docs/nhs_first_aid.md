# NHS Emergency First Aid & Acute Hemorrhage Protocol

## 1. Severe Hemorrhage & Bleeding Control
- **Direct Pressure**: Apply direct, uninterrupted firm pressure directly over the wound using sterile gauze or clean cloth.
- **Tourniquet**: If direct pressure fails to stop arterial spurting blood on limbs, apply a commercial tourniquet 2-3 inches proximal to the wound (avoiding joints).
- **Elevation**: Maintain pressure and keep the bleeding limb elevated above heart level if no fractures are suspected.

## 2. Traumatic Head & Neck Injury
- Do not move the patient's spine or neck.
- Monitor Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS). Loss of consciousness, persistent vomiting, or clear CSF fluid draining from nose/ears indicates acute basilar skull fracture. Urgent neurosurgical evaluation required.
