# CDC Emergency Department Triage Protocols & Red-Flag Criteria

## 1. Acute Myocardial Infarction & Coronary Distress
- **Primary Symptoms**: Retrosternal chest pain or substernal crushing pressure lasting >10 minutes, pain radiating to left shoulder, arm, neck, or jaw.
- **Associated Markers**: Diaphoresis, dyspnea, nausea, syncope.
- **Action**: Immediate EMS dispatch (911/112). Patient should remain seated. Administer non-enteric aspirin (160-325 mg chewed) if no contraindications exist. Prepare for immediate ACLS cardiac catheterization.

## 2. Stroke & Acute Neurological Deficits (FAST Protocol)
- **F - Facial Droop**: Asymmetry when smiling.
- **A - Arm Drift**: One arm drifts downward when raised.
- **S - Speech Difficulty**: Slurred, garbled, or absent speech.
- **T - Time to Act**: Emergency transport within 3-4.5 hours tPA thrombolytic window.
- **Red Flag**: Sudden severe "thunderclap" headache, hemiparesis, or unprovoked seizure requires emergency CT brain scan.
